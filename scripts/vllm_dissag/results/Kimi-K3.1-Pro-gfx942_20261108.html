What these cards are

Kimi-K3.1-Pro is a hybrid model: 69 KDA (gated delta) layers and 24 Gated DSA (sparse MLA with an indexer, top-k 2048) layers, 896 MXFP4 routed experts with top-16 routing, about 1.5 TB of weights and a 1M-token context. The cards serve it disaggregated over MoRIIO with two prefill nodes and one decode node (xP=2 yD=1, WIDE_EP=0). Kimi-K3.1-Pro-gfx942-dspark adds DSpark speculative decoding (the 5-layer K3.1 DSpark draft) on both legs.

Model runner V2 reads CUDAGRAPH_CAPTURE_SIZES (tokens) as the decode limit too, rounded to whole requests of 1 + 7 drafts = 8 tokens. With sizes up to 64 tokens only batches of 8 requests are graphed and c32 decode halves; the card captures up to 1024 tokens (128 requests). DSpark acceptance length is ~2.8–3.3 on mixed text and ~4.7 on reasoning text (~158 tok/s at c1). Without the draft, c1 decode is ~49 tok/s.

Known limitations

  • Validated on MI325X only; MI300X (192 GB) is untested and has less KV headroom.
  • The base card graphs decode up to 64 requests; above that decode runs without graphs.
  • Cold first-turn prefill dominates AgentX TTFT; the 2P1D topology and prefix retention address reuse, not cold prefill speed.
  • Experts run as int4 per-group-32 requantized from MXFP4 (lossy); accuracy is gated by GSM8K and NIAH above.
