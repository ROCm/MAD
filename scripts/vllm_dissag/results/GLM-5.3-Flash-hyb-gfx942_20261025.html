What these cards are

GLM-5.3-Flash-FP8-gfx942-hyb and GLM-5.3-Flash-MXFP4-gfx942-hyb run the same image and decode leg as the EP8 cards, with the prefill leg changed to TP4×DP2 (attention over 4 GPUs, experts still EP over all 8) and a KDA prefix-cache state kept every 17408 tokens. Each long prompt prefills about twice as fast and a session's second request resumes from the shared prefix instead of re-prefilling it. The trade-off is fewer concurrent cold prefills (2 engines instead of 8). Pick these cards for interactive long-context traffic with session reuse; pick the EP8 cards for many concurrent cold long prompts.

Expert parallelism and KV transfer

All four GLM-5.3-Flash-gfx942 cards use MoRIIO (RDMA READ) for the prefill → decode KV transfer and vLLM's allgather_reducescatter for the expert all-to-all (VLLM_ALL2ALL_BACKEND and DECODE_MORI_BACKEND, overriding the mori default of connector combo 3). Each MoE layer all-gathers the tokens of the 8 ranks before the experts and reduce-scatters the outputs after them, over RCCL; inside full decode CUDA graphs with uniform batches, vLLM uses AITER's custom all-gather / reduce-scatter kernels instead (default on). All-reduce appears only in the TP4 attention of the -hyb prefill.

MoRI-EP (mori_high_throughput prefill / mori_low_latency decode) is a tested opt-in, not the default (skills/glm53-flash-gfx942/Recipe_enable_MoriEP.md). EP8 cards, MXFP4:

MAD harness validation

The -hyb cards launch correctly through the MAD unified launcher (run_xPyD_models.slurm) with the image built from the Dockerfile. Confirmed per-leg:

  • Prefill: tensor_parallel_size: 4, block_size: 4352, prefix_cache_retention_interval: 17408 (agentic path)
  • Prefill MXFP4: "MXFP4 MoE experts converted exactly to FP8 128×128 blocks"
  • Decode: "MXFP4 MoE experts on AITER FlyDSL W4A16 with exact e2m1 decode", unchanged from EP8 cards

10-seed NIAH on MI300X: MXFP4-hyb 35.8/40, FP8-hyb 35.9/40 (equivalent; the 35K dip is the documented repetitive-filler caveat, present on both checkpoints and on the EP8 cards). AgentX conformance_256k: 428 measured requests, 0 measured errors, TTFT p50 6.89 s at concurrency 16.

Known limitations

  • MXFP4 per-user decode speed trails FP8 by 4–9% on MI300X at 4–8 sessions (the 4-bit FlyDSL W4A16 kernel's small-batch efficiency). Does not affect any TTFT result or capacity number. A variant with decode experts also converted to FP8 blocks closes the gap at the cost of the MXFP4 decode KV gain.
  • The -hyb cards have 2 prefill engines instead of 8: bursts of many concurrent cold long prompts are served faster by the EP8 cards.
